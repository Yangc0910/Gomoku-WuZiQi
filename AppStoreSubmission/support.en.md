# Skill Gomoku support

## How do I play?
Place a stone on an empty intersection. Make a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of five or more of your stones to win. Tap **Learn to play** on the home screen for three short interactive lessons.

## How do powers work?
Powers remove, restore, move, protect, or switch stones. A power uses your turn. Open **Try powers** on the home screen or the power guide during a game. Each power explains its legal targets, cooldown, and uses per game, and includes **Try it** practice.

## How do I start a game?
Choose **Play AI** or **Pass & Play**, choose your rules, and tap **Play now**. First-time players start with Easy AI and a 13 × 13 powers board. The home screen uses Easy AI and human-first defaults; Classic starts on 15 × 15. Custom Powers asks you to choose three powers before starting.

## Do I need the internet or an account?
No. All gameplay and AI analysis run offline. Profiles, avatars, settings, saved games, and stats stay on your device. There are no ads.

## How do I change the language?
The app follows your iOS preferred languages, including any language set specifically for this app. English and Simplified Chinese are supported; other unsupported preferences fall back to English. Open the app's page in iOS Settings to choose an app language. Your App Store country does not determine the in-game language.

## Can I resume a game or change its size?
Use **Resume game** on the home screen. A saved game keeps its original board size, including games created in older versions. New powers games use 13 × 13; new Classic games use 15 × 15.

## Can I change my avatar or delete my data?
Open **Players** to edit a nickname or choose an avatar from Photos or Camera. Camera access is optional and may be unavailable on a simulator. Delete a profile in its editor, or delete players and games in **Settings**. Deleting the app also removes local app data.
