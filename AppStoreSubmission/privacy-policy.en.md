# Skill Gomoku privacy policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Skill Gomoku is an offline board game for iPhone and iPad. The app does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data.

## Local information
Player nicknames, optional avatars, saved games, game history, and win/loss/draw statistics are stored on your device. Game preferences and lesson completion are also stored locally. Practice sessions do not contribute to game history or player statistics.

## Photos and camera
You may choose a photo or take a picture for a player avatar. This is optional. The app stores a local avatar image and does not upload it or share it with third parties.

## Network and tracking
The game requires no account, contains no ads or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not connect to a game server. AI analysis takes place entirely on your device. It does not track you across apps or websites. Opening the iOS Settings page does not send gameplay or profile data to us.

## Deletion
You can delete a player profile and its avatar from the profile editor, or delete player and game data from the app's Settings. Removing the app removes its local app data. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

## Children
The app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

## Changes and contact
If future versions add services that process data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated. For privacy questions, use the support page at https://github.com/Yangc0910/Gomoku-WuZiQi/blob/main/AppStoreSubmission/support.en.md.
